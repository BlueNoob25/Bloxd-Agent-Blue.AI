# Super Rank
Super Rank is a paywall in Bloxd.io that gives players certain cosmetics and an ad-free experience. It was added by Arthur on December 18, 2024.

## Perks
You will receive the following perks after you paid for the Super Rank:

 - The option to customize the colour of your name above your character’s head and in the chat
 - Capes as player customisation options
 - The Super Rank nametag next to your name above your character’s head, in the chat, and in the friends tab of other players that you are friends with
 - No advertisements
Other than that, Super Rank does not give you advantages, boosts or perks in any gamemodes.

Each Super Rank subscription lasts for 30 days (previously 90), and you need to renew the subscription if you want to have Super Rank for a longer period.

## Gifting
Since February 7, 2025, you can gift friends Super Rank on the condition that you pay for them.

## CrazyGames
From February 7, you can buy Super Rank on CrazyGames.

## Security Exploit Bounties
From December 11, 2025, you can receive one year of Super Rank for free if you reported a security flaw in Bloxd.io (bugs that could crash Bloxd for everyone), and 3-6 months for more minor issues.

## Name Tags
When you have Super Rank, there will be a “Super” next to your name in all the messages you send in the chat.

## Trivia
 - The Super Rank advertisement appears on the home page and in B-menus (the menu accessed by pressing the shopping cart icon or B).
 - Upon purchasing Super Rank, you will be redirected to https://bloxd.io/super-rank-welcome. You can also access the link without having Super Rank (but you will not get it from the link).
